# The six hero narrations, written by the team and checked

Written by the team and kept verbatim in scripts/authored_narrations.py. Re-checked by that script on 2026-11-07.

No watsonx call wrote these and no model is called for this file. Each line was read against data/raw/ by app/grounding.py as a goal call, in the persona and for the listening side its slot is written for, and then by the two acceptance rules a candidate meets: the places and the opener.

Every one of them is drafted with an AI assistant, edited by Shawn, verified by the grounding check. The model runs are in docs/narration_runs/ beside this file, which is where the pipeline's own output stays.

The billed count is what that one recording would cost, markup counted and whitespace not, which is the figure IBM charges.
