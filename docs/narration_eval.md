## What the two numbers mean

**First attempt grounded** is `Narrated.first_attempt_grounded`: the model's first answer passed `app/grounding.py` with no retry. A narration that failed twice and passed on the third attempt counts as a failure here, because the rate is about the prompt and the model, not about how many times the pipeline is willing to ask.

**Coverage** is the share of the key beats of the passage that the narration cited: the shot, every dribble past an opponent, and the foul for a penalty. `key_beats` in `app/narration_prompt.py` builds them, one per `Dribbled Past` record, with the `Dribble` on the other player's record joining the same beat so that citing either one counts. It lives in `app/` rather than in the measuring script because the fallback narration is built to reach every one of those beats, so the two have to be reading the same list.

**The template rows are not a model result.** The fallback is assembled by code out of the facts and cites every key beat by construction, so it scores 100% on all nine goals and a row below that would mean the fallback and the measure have drifted apart. It is in its own column for that reason: averaged in with the model's narrations it would lift the number by being a different thing, not by the model doing better.

The two measure different failures. The check refuses a narration that says what the data does not support. It has nothing to say about a narration that is true in every word and leaves out the whole run: on the 1986 54:18 goal the model has returned four true sentences that take facts 1 to 3 in order and then jump to the shot, passing the check and skipping all four dribbles. That narration is what coverage is for.

**Coverage says nothing on 4 of these 9 goals.** They have no dribble past an opponent and no penalty, so their only key beat is the shot, and `app/grounding.py` already refuses any narration whose last sentence does not cite it. Those goals read 100% whenever they pass at all, which is not evidence about the model. The key beats column is in the table so the two cases are told apart by looking. The ones with something to say are: `3750191 50:27 Maradona`, `3750191 54:18 Maradona`, `3869685 22:24 Cuccittini`, `3869685 79:24 Lottin`, `3869685 117:05 Lottin`.
